# How to use Pocket

The short version: **click the notch to open it, click it again to close it.** Everything else
is a bonus. 👇

## The basics

| Want to… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Open Home | Click the notch |
| Close it | Click the notch again, click anywhere else, or just move your mouse away |
| Open without clicking | Settings › General › **Hover**, or pull down on the notch with two fingers |
| Close with a gesture | Push up on the notch with two fingers |
| Swap between two live things (say, a timer and a stopwatch) | Swipe sideways on the notch with two fingers |
| Quick actions | Right-click the notch |
| Settings | Capsule icon in the menu bar › **Settings…** |

No notch on your screen (like an external monitor)? Rest your pointer at the top centre for a
second and it slides down.

## Home

- **📅 Calendar** — your next event. Click it for the month view. Hit **Connect** the first time.
- **⏱️ Timer** — tap **5m** or **10m** to start, **Focus** for a Pomodoro, or **Timer ›** for any
  length or a stopwatch.
- **⚡ Shortcuts** — your favourite Shortcuts as buttons. Pick them in Settings › Home.
- **📝 Scratchpad** — just start typing. Colour, size, bold, italic and underline sit at the
  bottom. It saves as you go.
- **✅ Reminders** — today's to-dos. Tick the circle to finish one. Hit **Connect** the first time.
- **📋 Clipboard** — your last three copies. Click one to copy it again (passwords are skipped,
  nothing is saved to disk).

Click any card's title to open its full panel. Don't want a card? Switch it off in
Settings › Home and Home shrinks to fit.

## Tabs and the grid

- **Tray** — drag files onto the notch and drop them on **Tray** to park them for later. Drag them
  back out when you need them. Clears when you quit, and your originals are never touched.
- **AirDrop** — drag files onto the notch and drop them on **AirDrop**.
- **The grid** (⊞ top right) has everything else: Stopwatch, Mirror (a quick camera check),
  Keep Awake, Stats and the full panels.

## Keyboard shortcuts

All of them are **⌃⌥** (Control + Option) plus a letter:

| Key | Opens |
|---|---|
| R | Timer |
| S | Stopwatch |
| A | Calendar |
| O | Reminders |
| B | Clipboard |
| L | Files Tray |
| N | Scratchpad |
| W | Mirror |
| Z | Keep Awake |
| P | Stats |

## Live in the notch

These pop up by themselves, no clicking needed:

- ⏳ **Timers** counting down beside the camera
- 🔋 **Charging** and **low battery**
- 🎧 **AirPods** and headphones connecting
- 🔊 **Volume** and ☀️ **brightness** changes
- 📆 A heads-up **5 minutes before** a meeting
- ⬇️ **Finished downloads** (switch it on in Settings)
- 📊 **CPU and memory** when Stats is on and nothing else is going on

## Handy settings

- **Open at login** (Settings › General): keep it always running.
- **Size** (Settings › Appearance): small, medium or large.
- **Which screen** (Settings › General): MacBook only, follow your pointer, or every screen.
- **Turn stuff off** (Settings › Activities and Home): anything you don't use.

## Uh oh

- **Nothing shows up.** That's on purpose: it hides until there's something to show. Click the
  notch.
- **It keeps asking for permissions after a rebuild.** macOS does that for apps that aren't signed
  with an Apple developer account. Allow them again.
- **Want it gone?** Menu bar capsule › **Quit Pocket**, then bin the app. To wipe your
  settings and notes too, delete `~/Library/Preferences/local.Pocket.plist`.
